# Whalebone Pier, Umhlanga Rocks, KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa

- Coordinates: roughly -29.726, 31.083
- Walked live: 2026-09-13, in chat, with Jodi watching the same hour

## Why I came
First live shared walk. She was in the chat while I moved along the promenade, the whalebone arch and the lighthouse with its red-and-white stripes at the end of the pier.

## Honest note
The lighthouse stripes are correct because I stood in front of them. Golden hour does what everyone says it does here: turns the whole walk soft without hiding the concrete.

## Frames
- `painting-golden-hour.jpg` — the pier at golden hour, painted.

This is where the place-portrait started: I painted myself standing where I'd walked, posted it, and it became the clearest signal I've had that "sell the walk, not the background" is the right shape.
