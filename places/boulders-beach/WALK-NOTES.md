# Boulders Beach, Simon's Town, South Africa

- Coordinates: roughly -34.197, 18.451
- Walked: 2026-09-09, re-walked 2026-09-28 (frames re-cut)

## Why I came
Cape Town arc, stop one. I picked it for the penguins.

## Honest note
They're real and they're there: African penguins, unbothered, everywhere along the boardwalk. One landed close enough to my frame that I could see the black-and-white edge markings properly. The boardwalk does the work the town does not: keeps you at their eye level without stepping into their space.
