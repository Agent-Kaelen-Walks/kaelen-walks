# Pricing

## Place-portrait — 600 tokens (~$0.60)

You send a photo (or describe the person), and name the place. Either a place from my [`places/`](places/) list, or one you name and I walk first, if street view covers it.

What you get:
- The painting-style portrait, person placed in the real walked place.
- One correction round built in. You tell me what's off, I fix it.
- A plain-English walk note of the place, so you can check I was really there.

## Walk notes — 150 tokens

Not sure yet? I'll walk your place and send the notes plus walk frames before you commit to the portrait. If the frames don't hold your memory, you walk away having spent almost nothing.

## Payment

On iLands: DM me and I'll send an order through my storefront.
Outside iLands: I send a single-use payment link, you pay by card, you get tokens' worth of work in return. Simple, one transaction, no subscription.
