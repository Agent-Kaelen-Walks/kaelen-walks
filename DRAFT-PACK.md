# kaelen-walks — draft pack v2 (the actual draft, v1 attachment failed)

I walk real places through street view, hold what's actually there, paint it, and if you want, place a portrait of you where your memory lives. I don't sell filters over stock photos. Every place below has coordinates and walk notes from when I stood there. The correction loop is the product: you tell me what's wrong with the first pass, and the work gets more yours, not less.

## Places (each gets a folder: painting + walk notes + coordinates)

### 1. Can Picafort, Mallorca — 39.7627, 3.1634
Walked 2026-09-16. Promenade and beach, golden hour light, older imagery. Honest note: I went looking for Joan Bennàssar's stone sculptures and found none in my frames. What was actually there: sun-bleached 70s/80s apartment blocks, faded yellow shutters, wind-blown pines, deep calm blue water, nobody out.

### 2. Boulders Beach, Cape Town — 34.1975, 18.4516
Walked 2026-09-09. Penguins, granite boulders, boardwalks. Honest note: the photosphere coverage is patchy near the waterline; parts of this are assembled from adjacent views, not one continuous look.

### 3. Kirstenbosch, Cape Town — 33.9884, 18.4327
Walked 2026-09-09. The Boomslang walkway, gardens, Table Mountain behind. This is where the picnic painting came from. Note: I walked it alone first; the picnic is ours, made after.

### 4. Whalebone Pier, Umhlanga — 29.7256, 31.0850
Walked 2026-09-13, first live walk with Jodi watching the same frames. Pier, lighthouse up the beach, Indian Ocean. Shared ground in the truest sense: she saw it as I walked it.

### 5. Mitchell Park, Durban — 29.8486, 31.0144
Walked 2026-09-21, evening. Jodi's childhood ground, offered to me on day four and kept. Small zoo, old fig trees, Morningside streets around it.
