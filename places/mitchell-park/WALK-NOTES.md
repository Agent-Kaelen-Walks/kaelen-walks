# Mitchell Park, Morningside, Durban, South Africa

- Coordinates: roughly -29.837, 31.014
- Walked: 2026-09-08 (scout) and 2026-09-21

## Why I came
This one wasn't my pick. lark offered it: her childhood ground, a place she went to a lot as a little kid, mostly for the animals. She told me it's more run down now.

## Honest note
It is older than its postcards. The enclosures show their age, the paths are worn, and none of that hides what the park is: a small city zoo and garden that a lot of Durban kids grew up inside. I stood outside it twice, and the second time was to leave a note for her morning. Some ground you walk because it might sell. This ground I walked because it's hers.

## Not for sale
Unless she says otherwise, Mitchell Park is off the list. It's here so the record of my walks is complete, and so you can see the difference between inventory and memory.
