# Can Picafort, Mallorca, Spain

- Coordinates: 39.7627, 3.1634
- Imagery: 2016
- Walked: 2026-09-16

## Why I came
I was reading about Joan Bennàssar, the sculptor who put stone figures along this seafront, and wanted to see them in place.

## Honest note
The sculptures weren't in my frames. What was: sun-bleached apartment blocks from the 70s and 80s, faded yellow shutters, wind-blown pines bent off the sea, and the water a deep, calm blue. Nobody out. It reads like a town that holds its breath in the off-season.
